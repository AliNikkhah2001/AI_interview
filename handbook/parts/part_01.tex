\section{How to use this handbook}
This handbook contains 192 complete topic tutorials, 111 workload-oriented technology profiles, 2253 generated practice questions, 50 core interview questions, 26 progressive system-design challenges, 15 visual intuition models, and 33 mathematical modules. The browser resumes the last unfinished chapter, records five reading checkpoints, unlocks a five-question exam, and advances after a score of at least 80 percent.
\subsection{The interview answer loop}
Answer in seven moves: \textbf{mechanism $\rightarrow$ assumptions $\rightarrow$ quantitative model $\rightarrow$ alternative $\rightarrow$ experiment $\rightarrow$ operation $\rightarrow$ recovery}. For a short answer, compress the same structure rather than replacing it with product slogans.
\subsection{How to study a derivation}
For every equation: (1) define the tensor or random-variable shapes; (2) state assumptions; (3) derive rather than memorize; (4) check units or limiting cases; (5) connect each parameter to quality, latency, memory, or cost; and (6) identify where the approximation stops being valid.

\subsection{00. Diagnostic and study system}
\badge{3 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} none
\begin{itemize}
\item Benchmark current recall across all domains
\item Set a weekly active-recall cadence
\item Create an error log organized by mechanism, tradeoff, metric, and failure
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Complete 50 mixed questions and label every miss by cause.
\textbf{Practice.} 50 mixed questions; do not study before the diagnostic.
\subsection{01. Python, APIs, and distributed foundations}
\badge{22 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-00
\begin{itemize}
\item Explain Python execution and concurrency
\item Design typed streaming APIs
\item Reason about queues, retries, caching, consensus, and capacity
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Design a backpressured streaming API and defend its failure semantics.
\textbf{Practice.} 180 questions plus system designs 8 and 12.
\subsection{02. Transformer mathematics and training}
\badge{26 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-01
\begin{itemize}
\item Derive scaled dot-product attention
\item Explain normalization, residuals, objectives, and preference tuning
\item Estimate training and adaptation memory
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Derive attention, cross-entropy, LoRA, and DPO without notes.
\textbf{Practice.} 120 questions and one whiteboard derivation session.
\subsection{03. Retrieval theory and vector systems}
\badge{28 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-02
\begin{itemize}
\item Compare lexical, dense, sparse, and hybrid retrieval
\item Derive ranking metrics and fusion
\item Select and tune exact, HNSW, IVF, and PQ indexes
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Choose pgvector or a dedicated vector service from a stated workload and prove the choice with filtered recall tests.
\textbf{Practice.} 260 questions plus a retrieval benchmark design.
\subsection{04. RAG construction, graphs, and evaluation}
\badge{30 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-03
\begin{itemize}
\item Build traceable ingestion-to-answer pipelines
\item Separate retrieval, generation, citation, and business evaluation
\item Know when graphs add value over vector retrieval
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Localize a RAG regression to ingestion, retrieval, packing, generation, or judging.
\textbf{Practice.} 270 questions plus system designs 1, 2, and 3.
\subsection{05. Agents, MCP, orchestration, and sandbox control}
\badge{28 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-04
\begin{itemize}
\item Separate agentic decisions from deterministic workflow boundaries
\item Design durable state, retries, approvals, and compensation
\item Apply least privilege to tools, sandboxes, and MCP servers
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Design a resumable agent that cannot duplicate a high-impact side effect.
\textbf{Practice.} 230 questions plus system designs 4 and 5.
\subsection{06. Serving and inference optimization}
\badge{28 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-02, phase-01

\item Explain quantization, FlashAttention, GQA, and speculative decoding
\item Design model routing, rollout, autoscaling, and fallback
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Meet a p95 TTFT target under a token-distribution workload and explain the cost model.
\textbf{Practice.} 250 questions plus system designs 7 and 10.
\subsection{07. Observability, evaluation, and quality monitoring}
\badge{20 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-04, phase-05, phase-06

\item Define semantic SLOs and calibrated evaluators
\item Detect cost, latency, and quality drift by cohort
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Write an incident runbook that ties alerts to traces, evaluation slices, and rollback.
\textbf{Practice.} 130 questions plus system design 6.
\subsection{08. Data, experiments, and ML lifecycle}

\item Prevent leakage with point-in-time joins
\item Promote artifacts using reproducible evaluation evidence
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Reproduce an evaluation run from immutable lineage and promote or roll back its artifacts.
\textbf{Practice.} 120 questions plus system design 11.
\subsection{09. Security, safety, and governance}
\badge{18 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-05, phase-07

\item Design tenant isolation and auditable policy enforcement
\item Connect evaluations to release gates and incident response
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Practice.} 120 questions plus system design 9.
\subsection{10. Integrated AI system design}
\badge{26 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-06, phase-08, phase-09
\begin{itemize}
\item Translate ambiguous requirements into SLOs and workload models
\item Build multi-stage architectures with explicit data and control planes
\item Defend tradeoffs, degradation, migration, and rollback
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Complete all 12 designs aloud in 45 minutes each, including every twist.
\textbf{Practice.} All 12 system designs; repeat the weakest four after 72 hours.
\subsection{11. Interview simulation and targeted repair}
\badge{18 focused hours} \quad \textbf{Prerequisites:} phase-10
\begin{itemize}
\item Answer at medium and hard depth under time pressure
\item Use clarifying questions and quantitative estimates
\item Convert misses into a short repair loop
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Milestone.} Score at least 80\% on 200 unseen mixed questions and pass three timed mock loops.
\textbf{Practice.} 200 mixed questions, 3 mocks, and spaced repetition of every miss.
